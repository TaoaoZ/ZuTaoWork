Introduction

Konpo’s mission spanned a dual makeover — first, a marketing website that mirrors the sophistication of HAIR&SKIN's treatments, and second, a mobile experience tailored for clients, ensuring a seamless post-treatment journey. We created a design that’s scalable for the hyper growth state that the startup was at. And meticulously crafted a reliable Design System, setting the stage for an engaging user experience and increase in conversion.

Konpo collaborated closely with the product team to consistently enhance the App's user experience through the implementation of A/B testing and spearheading design sprints.

The outcomes include a visually engaging website, an intuitive app interface, and a cohesive brand identity that exudes sophistication and precision.
