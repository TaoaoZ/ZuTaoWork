Nantucket Foundation
Interactive Maps for the island of Nantucket.

We partnered up with Nantucket Foundation that aim at bringing the allure of Nantucket's hidden trails to your fingertips. The challenge at hand: crafting an app that not only navigates the island's wild beauty but also captures the essence of every adventurer's spirit.

The scope encompassed redefining how users experience the island's enchanting and preserved trails. We designed and developed an iOS and Android app, ensuring seamless navigation along its trails, community-uploaded photos and clear filters so everyone can find what they like.

This turned into the compass for every Nantucket visit, offering not just navigation but a tailored adventure experience. Our deliverables include an intuitive interactive map, a revamped interface for easy exploration, and a collaborative platform for sharing and celebrating island escapades.

What is our approach to product design?

Collaboration, understanding and more collaboration. We're committed to understanding both our users and our clients' organizations, allowing us to address genuine challenges they face. Collaboration lies at the heart of our process, as we immerse ourselves in problems and collaborate closely to devise user-centric solutions. Our dedication to excellence extends to every aspect of our work, from internal communication to UX and UI design. We prioritize fostering strong relationships with developers, management, and end users alike, ensuring that our designs not only meet but exceed expectations and deliver tangible value. Beyond pretty pixels

What do you deliver?

From UX design, which focuses on enhancing user experiences, to UI design, which centers on creating intuitive interfaces, we cover every aspect of the design process. Additionally, our expertise extends to motion design, adding dynamic elements to enhance user engagement and visual storytelling. Furthermore, we develop robust design systems that provide consistency and coherence across all facets of our clients' projects.

What does the timeline look like?

Product design tends to be an open ended engagement driven by the roadmap. But these are longer-term timelines where dive deep into the problems we're trying to solve. We aim for engagements that span over several quarters.

‍

What tools do you work with?

FIGMA ALL DAY. No joke, its our second home. But other than that we use the full adobe creative suite along with AI tools to support periphery needs. Then depending on the projects, we like to do a lot of user testing, so we rely on the usual suspects for that.

Also slack and notion for smooth communications and documentation.

‍

What's the general price range?

Product work is generally conducted through retainers, as we firmly believe that successful product design demands a profound understanding, iterative refinement, and robust collaboration with project managers and developers. Through experience, we've realized that short-term engagements don't align with our approach to delivering optimal solutions. We prioritize deeply engaging with users and immersing ourselves in the intricacies of the problems we aim to solve, ensuring that we provide maximum value to our clients. Generally speaking, our engagement rates start at 15,000 USD per month, reflecting our commitment to delivering high-quality, impactful design services.

Why us?

We’re passionate about what we do, we don’t settle, and we understand what GREAT work looks like to strive towards creating best-in-class design.

If you’re looking for a team that just wants to push pixels, we’re not for you.  If you’re looking for a committed team that will push the envelope and aims to deliver value to the organization and the end user, we're a match :)
